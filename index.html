About

I am an AI Research Scientist at Meta working on LLM alignment and agents. I am based in London, UK.

In 2023, I completed my PhD at PSL University (Université Paris Dauphine) and Meta (FAIR) in Paris, advised by Nicolas Usunier (FAIR), Jamal Atif and Jérôme Lang (PSL University, CNRS).

My PhD focused on fairness in recommender systems and blends economic concepts with convex optimization and bandit techniques. An overview can be found in this blogpost.

Prior to my PhD, I received an MSc in Social Data Science from the University of Oxford, an MSc/BSc in Applied Mathematics from Ecole Polytechnique (Diplôme d'Ingénieur) , and a BA in Philosophy from Université Paris Nanterre.
